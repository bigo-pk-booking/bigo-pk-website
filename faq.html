Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about using the BADASS BIGO PK SLOTS booking platform.

1. What is BADASS BIGO PK SLOTS?

BADASS is an independent community platform that helps BIGO hosts and agencies coordinate PK match booking requests, review booking information, and follow match schedules. It is not an official BIGO LIVE service.

2. How do I find an available PK slot?

Open the booking page, review the schedule, and look for a date and time marked as available. Booked or unavailable times should not be selected.

3. What information do I need to submit a booking?

Provide the host details requested by the booking form, including the BIGO host name and BIGO ID, then check the selected date and time before submitting.

4. What happens after I submit a booking?

A successful submission displays a confirmation message. The request is then subject to review by an authorized BADASS administrator. Keep your booking details so you can follow up if needed.

5. Is a submitted booking automatically approved?

No. Submission means the request was received. Authorized administrators review requests and manage booking status in the admin panel.

6. When will opponent information appear?

Opponent information appears after it has been assigned to the booking. If it has not yet been assigned, check the booking details or follow the community's update channels.

7. Where can I check my PK schedule?

Use the My OPK area on the booking website to look up your booking information and check the schedule features available to your account.

8. How can I receive community updates?

Use the Telegram community link provided on the BADASS Contact page for announcements and booking-related updates. Messenger is also listed there for general questions.

9. What should I do if my booking details are incorrect?

Contact the BADASS team through the official contact options listed on the Contact page. Include the relevant booking date and BIGO ID, but never share your password or payment credentials.

10. Is BADASS affiliated with BIGO LIVE?

No. BADASS is independently operated and is not owned, sponsored, endorsed, or officially operated by BIGO LIVE or its affiliates. BIGO names and marks belong to their respective owners.

Important: This FAQ is general guidance. Actual slot availability, booking status, and opponent assignments are determined by the live booking system and authorized administrators. Never share your account password or sensitive payment information.
