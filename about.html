About BADASS

BIGO PK SLOTS · Battle with honor. Stand as one.

BADASS BIGO PK SLOTS is an independent community platform created to help BIGO hosts and agencies coordinate PK match bookings in one organized place.

What the platform does

  • Shows available PK booking slots and schedule information.
  • Allows hosts to submit booking requests and look up their booking details.
  • Helps authorized administrators review bookings and maintain opponent information.
  • Shares community announcements and support links.

Our community

We believe every match is better when hosts support one another. Our goal is to make booking coordination clearer, more organized, and easier for the community.

Independent platform notice: BADASS is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official service of BIGO LIVE or its parent companies.

For booking help or community updates, visit our Contact Us page.
