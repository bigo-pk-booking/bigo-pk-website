A Practical Guide to Preparing Your PK Booking

BADASS BIGO PK SLOTS · Booking preparation and scheduling

A smooth PK booking starts before you press submit. Checking the schedule, confirming host details, and keeping a clear record of your request can help prevent avoidable delays and scheduling mistakes.

Important: This guide explains good preparation practices. It does not guarantee that a requested slot will be approved or that an opponent will be assigned. Actual availability and booking status are controlled by the live BADASS booking system and authorized administrators.

1. Check the schedule before choosing a time

Start with the live booking schedule and identify a date and time that is marked available. Do not rely on an old screenshot, a forwarded message, or a schedule you saved earlier: another request may have changed availability since then.

If the time you want is already marked booked or unavailable, choose another open slot rather than submitting a duplicate request. If the status is unclear, ask the BADASS team to confirm it before making plans with your opponent.

2. Confirm the host information carefully

Before completing the form, check the host display name and BIGO ID directly against the host's current profile. A small typo can make a booking harder to identify or connect to the right person.

  • Use the correct host display name.
  • Copy the BIGO ID carefully and check every digit.
  • Review any agency or contact details requested by the form.
  • Only include remarks that are relevant to coordinating the match.

3. Agree on the match details before submitting

When possible, confirm the intended date, time, time zone, and opponent with the other host before sending the request. If hosts are in different countries, write the time zone clearly in your own coordination messages. A time that looks correct on one person's device may be interpreted differently by someone elsewhere.

Keep the booking form's requested time and the time agreed with the other host consistent. If plans change, contact the administrators instead of assuming that an edited message changes the saved booking.

4. Review everything before pressing submit

Pause for a final check. Confirm the selected slot, host name, BIGO ID, and other required details. If the form shows a confirmation or summary screen, read it before completing the request.

Pre-submission checklist
  • Correct date and time selected
  • Slot currently shown as available
  • Host name and BIGO ID checked
  • Opponent or agency details accurate, where requested
  • Remarks are clear and do not contain sensitive information

5. Understand what a confirmation means

A successful submission means the website received your request. It does not necessarily mean the booking has been approved, finalized, or matched with an opponent. Follow the booking status shown by the website and wait for any required administrator review.

Keep the information needed to look up your request. If you contact support, provide the booking date and relevant BIGO ID so the team can identify the correct entry.

6. Keep a record of changes

If the date, time, host, or opponent changes after submission, make sure the authorized administrator updates the booking record. Do not rely only on a private chat or verbal agreement as proof that the website's schedule has changed.

After an update, check the booking details again. This helps both hosts work from the same information and reduces the chance of someone following an outdated schedule.

7. Protect your account and personal information

A PK booking does not require you to share your BIGO password. Never send passwords, one-time verification codes, payment-card details, or government identity documents through booking remarks or community messages. Use only the contact channels listed on the BADASS Contact page.

8. Be considerate when coordinating with another host

Clear, respectful communication makes scheduling easier. Confirm details early, notify the other host if plans change, and avoid making promises about approval or opponent assignment until the booking status confirms it. If a disagreement occurs, ask an administrator to review the booking record.

9. Where to get help

For instructions on the website workflow, visit How It Works and the FAQ. For a question about a specific request, use the official channels on the Contact page.
