Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BADASS BIGO PK SLOTS (“BADASS,” “we,” “our,” or “the website”) handles information when you visit or use this independent community PK booking platform.

Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government ID numbers, or other highly sensitive information through this website. We do not need your BIGO password to arrange a PK booking.

1. Information we may collect

  • Booking details: information entered for a booking, which may include BIGO display name and ID, host level, agency name and Telegram contact, requested date and time, opponent details, event information, remarks, and booking status.
  • Administrator account information: administrator email address and account/access identifiers used to authenticate and manage the website.
  • Technical and browser information: information automatically processed by the hosting, database, and API services needed to deliver the website and protect its operation.
  • Local browser storage: the website may save the most recently used booking lookup ID in your browser so you can retrieve your booking more easily. You can clear this through your browser’s site-data settings.

2. How information is used

Information is used to receive and manage PK slot requests, display and maintain booking schedules, help administrators verify and update bookings, respond to support requests, prevent duplicate or invalid bookings, and maintain website security and reliability.

3. Service providers

The website uses third-party infrastructure to operate. The current application is configured to use Firebase services for authentication and database functions, and Cloudflare-hosted API services for booking and related administration functions. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

Links to Telegram and Messenger are provided for community updates and support. If you open those services, your interaction is governed by the relevant provider’s privacy policy and settings.

4. Sharing and visibility

Booking information may be accessible to authorized BADASS administrators and may be used in the booking calendar or related website features. Do not include private information in booking remarks or contact fields unless it is necessary for the booking. We do not sell personal information.

5. Advertising and analytics

As of the effective date above, this policy does not represent Google AdSense advertising as active on the website. If advertising, analytics, or other tracking services are added later, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant technologies and choices before or when they are introduced. Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies where applicable.

6. Data retention and security

Booking and administrator information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to operate the booking service, maintain records, resolve disputes, and meet legitimate operational or legal needs. We use third-party platform security features and access controls, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and requests

You may choose not to submit booking information, clear locally stored website data in your browser, or contact the administrators to ask a question about information associated with a booking. We may need enough details to locate the relevant record and verify that a request is legitimate.

8. Children

This website is intended for people who are legally able to use the relevant BIGO and online services. Do not use the booking service if you are below the applicable minimum age for those services.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the website’s features or service providers change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest revision.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the official BADASS contact channels. Please do not send passwords or sensitive identity documents.
